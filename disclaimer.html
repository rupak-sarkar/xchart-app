1. Nature of Service

xchart.in (“the Platform”) is an experimental, educational technology project that demonstrates the application of artificial intelligence, machine learning, and quantitative analysis techniques to publicly available stock market data from the National Stock Exchange of India (NSE) and Bombay Stock Exchange (BSE).

The Platform displays:

  • Technical indicators (RSI, MACD, Bollinger Bands, SuperTrend, ADX, Ichimoku, SMA, EMA, VWAP)
  • AI-generated composite scores derived from algorithmic models
  • Backtesting results from historical simulations
  • Market regime assessments based on breadth data
  • News sentiment analysis using natural language processing

These outputs are algorithmic in nature and are generated automatically without human editorial judgment or personalized assessment of any individual’s financial situation, risk tolerance, or investment objectives.

4. AI & Technology Disclosure

In compliance with the spirit of SEBI (Research Analysts) (Third Amendment) Regulations, 2024, which requires disclosure of AI usage, we provide full transparency about the technology used:

4.1 Models & Algorithms

  • FinBERT (ProsusAI/finbert) — Pre-trained NLP model for financial sentiment analysis of news headlines
  • Technical Indicators — RSI(14), MACD(12,26,9), Bollinger Bands(22,2), SuperTrend, ADX(14), Ichimoku, SMA(9/22/200), EMA(9/21), VWAP
  • Composite Scoring — Weighted combination: Technical (65%), Macro/Sector (16%), Fundamental (11%), News (10%)
  • Market Regime — Based on Nifty 50 returns and market breadth indicators
  • Backtesting Engine — Historical walk-forward simulation with ATR-based stop-losses

4.2 Known Limitations

  • Models are trained on historical data and may not adapt to unprecedented market events
  • FinBERT was trained primarily on English-language financial text and may misinterpret Indian market context
  • Technical indicators are lagging by nature and cannot predict future price movements
  • Composite scores involve subjective weight assignments that may not be optimal
  • Backtest results suffer from survivorship bias, look-ahead bias, and may not reflect real-world slippage, commissions, or taxes
  • The system runs on a daily batch process and does NOT provide real-time analysis

4.3 No Human Override

All signals, scores, and classifications are generated entirely by automated algorithms without human editorial review or override. No human analyst reviews individual stock recommendations before publication.
