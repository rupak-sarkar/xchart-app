🚫 Not Investment Advice

xchart.in is an analytical and educational tool. It does not provide investment advice, stock recommendations, buy/sell signals, portfolio management, or any financial advisory services as defined by the Securities and Exchange Board of India (SEBI).

All outputs — including scores, directions, composite values, momentum readings, and backtest results — are mathematical computations based on publicly available historical data and standard technical indicators. They are presented for informational and educational purposes only.

📊 Nature of the Platform

  • xchart.in is an analytical tool that applies mathematical models (moving averages, RSI, Bollinger Bands, MACD, and other standard technical indicators) to publicly available stock price data.
  • The platform performs backtesting — testing user-configured rules against historical data to show how those rules would have performed in the past.
  • Premium analytics uses multi-layer composite scoring (technical, fundamental, macro, and sentiment analysis) to generate analytical readings.
  • All computations are deterministic and rule-based. No proprietary predictions or forecasts are made.

⚠️ Risk Disclosure
